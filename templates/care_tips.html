1. The "Top-2-Inches" Watering Rule

Overwatering is the #1 cause of houseplant demise. Rather than watering on a fixed calendar schedule, always insert your index finger 2 inches into the topsoil. If soil feels completely dry and crumbly, water thoroughly until it trickles out the drainage holes. If it feels cool and moist, wait another 2-3 days.

Pro-Tip: Empty the drainage saucer 15 minutes after watering. Never let plant roots sit in standing water to prevent root rot.

2. Deciphering Sunlight Needs: Direct vs. Bright Indirect

Bright Indirect Light: Bright enough to comfortably read a book without artificial lighting, but where the sun's harsh rays do not directly heat the leaf surface (e.g. Monstera, Snake Plant, Peace Lily).
Full Direct Sunlight: Unfiltered rays hitting the foliage for 5 to 7+ hours daily (e.g. Bougainvillea, Hibiscus, Dwarf Mango, Adenium).

Symptom Check: Scorched brown crispy patches mean too much direct sun; pale elongated stems and dropped lower leaves mean the plant needs more ambient light.

3. Soil Aeration & Repotting Essentials

Greeni5 potted plants arrive in nutrient-balanced coco-peat and vermicompost blends designed for immediate healthy growth. Wait 7 to 14 days after transit before repotting so the plant adapts to your home environment. When upgrading pots, only choose a planter 1-2 inches wider in diameter.

4. Organic Feeding & Fertilization

Feed your plants during active growth months (Spring to Autumn) with organic seaweed extract or balanced slow-release vermicompost once every 3-4 weeks. In deep winter, reduce feeding as plant metabolism naturally rests.
