Shipping & 7-Day Guarantee Policy

Careful packing & rapid dispatch across India

1. Eco-Safe Plant Transit Packaging

Every living plant is thoroughly hydrated before packaging and shielded inside custom corrugated shockproof planters with organic coco-peat root ball moisture wraps. This ensures your botanical companion stays refreshed for up to 8 days in transit.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Metro Cities: Delivered within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Rest of India: Delivered within 3 to 6 business days.
  • Orders placed on Saturday evening and Sunday are dispatched fresh on Monday morning to minimize weekend hub delays.

3. Free Shipping Eligibility

Orders totaling ₹999 or more automatically qualify for FREE Express Courier Shipping across India. For orders below ₹999, a nominal standard flat delivery fee of ₹99 is applied at checkout.

4. 7-Day Live Plant Replacement Guarantee

In rare events of transit mishandling (such as root breakage or severe drying), our 7-Day Replacement Guarantee protects you 100%. Simply visit your order tracking page or our Damage Claims portal, upload a quick snapshot, and we will courier a brand new healthy plant immediately at zero extra cost.
