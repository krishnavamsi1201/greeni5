Our Mission

Bringing Forests Back Into Modern Homes

Founded with a deep reverence for nature, Greeni5 cultivates over 100 species of acclimatized plants, transforming concrete rooms into thriving sanctuaries of clean air and tranquility.

From Sapling to Doorstep

Every specimen at Greeni5 begins life in our climate-regulated polyhouses in Bengaluru. Raised by seasoned botanists without synthetic growth accelerants, our plants develop robust fibrous root networks that transition seamlessly into home pots.

Engineered Transit Protection

We custom-designed breathable honeycomb cardboard cases that lock the soil in place and protect fragile stems against bumps during transit. Your plant arrives as fresh and hydrated as if you picked it directly from our greenhouse.
