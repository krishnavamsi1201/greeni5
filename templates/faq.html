Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, safe plant delivery, care, and returns.

How do you safely ship live plants across cities?

We developed proprietary transit enclosures utilizing breathable corrugated cardboard cases and soil retention collars. Pots are pre-hydrated with moisture-retaining coco gel to guarantee up to 7 days of safe transit life.

What is Greeni5's 7-Day Replacement Guarantee?

If any plant arrives damaged or unhealthy, simply send a photo to care@greeni5.com within 7 days of delivery. We will immediately dispatch a free replacement plant or issue a 100% full refund with zero hassle.

Should I immediately repot my plant when it arrives?

We strongly recommend letting your new plant rest in its nursery grower pot for at least 7 to 10 days in an indirect light corner. This lets the plant acclimatize to your local temperature and humidity without root shock.

What are your delivery timelines and shipping charges?

Deliveries take 2 to 5 business days depending on your postal code. We offer FREE shipping on all orders over ₹999. For smaller orders, a standard delivery fee of ₹99 applies.

Do you accept Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes! We accept Cash on Delivery as well as digital UPI and cards across all serviceable pincodes in India.
